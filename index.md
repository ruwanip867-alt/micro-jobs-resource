# Micro Jobs: A Practical Resource Hub for Online Task Workers

Micro jobs are small online assignments that can often be completed remotely. They may include research, data-related activities, content tasks, testing, surveys, or other digital work. The requirements and availability can vary between platforms, so workers should read each task description carefully before accepting it.

Here is a practical resource list for people researching different micro jobs platforms and online work opportunities.

## 1. GainWorkers

GainWorkers provides task-based online work where some assignments require workers to submit screenshots or other proof of completion. Workers should carefully follow the employer's instructions and keep the required evidence before submitting a task. [GainWorkers](https://www.gainworkers.com) is one option for people looking for proof-based micro jobs.

## 2. Clickworker

Clickworker offers different types of online assignments, including data-related and research activities. Available work can depend on a worker's location, qualifications, and current demand. Reading the instructions before starting is important.

## 3. Toloka

Toloka provides crowdsourcing tasks involving areas such as data evaluation and other online activities. Individual tasks can have different requirements, so workers should review the instructions and completion conditions first.

## 4. Prolific

Prolific is focused mainly on research studies. Participants may receive study invitations depending on their profile and the eligibility requirements of individual researchers. Availability can vary over time.

## 5. SproutGigs

SproutGigs is a microjob marketplace where users can find small online assignments. Each job can have its own requirements and proof rules, making it important to understand the instructions before accepting the work.

## 6. Amazon Mechanical Turk

Amazon Mechanical Turk provides a marketplace for Human Intelligence Tasks. Workers can find different types of assignments, although access to particular tasks can depend on qualifications and other requirements.

## 7. Appen

Appen has been associated with projects involving data collection, annotation, evaluation, and other technology-related work. Project availability and qualification requirements can vary according to location and project needs.

## What to Check Before Starting Micro Jobs

Workers should consider more than the advertised task amount. Completion deadlines, proof requirements, review windows, qualification rules, and withdrawal conditions are all useful factors to check before starting. Keeping screenshots of required proof can also help workers maintain accurate records.

Micro jobs should not be viewed as guaranteed income. Task availability and approval times can change, and some submitted work may remain under review before payment becomes available in a wallet. A careful approach is to start with clearly explained assignments, follow the platform rules, and only accept work that can realistically be completed within the required timeframe.
